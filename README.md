# Before You Eat

**Before You Eat** is an attempt at making macro tracking as easy and fast as possible. The idea is to be able to quickly log any meal and its macros from anywhere without having to download anything or sign up/sign in anywhere. By having the app on a website with local browser storage in the form of cookies and an optional bigger persistent database storage linked to a Google account, you can choose how you want the app to store your macros.
